The Art of Henna

More Than a Design — Part of the Celebration.

Henna is more than a design — it's part of the celebration.

I create detailed, custom henna inspired by traditional motifs while incorporating elements that make each bride's design personal. Using only high-quality organic henna, every design is crafted to achieve a rich, deep stain.

From intimate engagement celebrations in Clifton to large bridal mehndis in Paterson and Eid gatherings across Wayne and Totowa — I bring artistry and care to every event.
