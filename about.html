About

Meet Afreen

Makeup artist. Henna artist. Artist at heart.

I'm Afreen Fatima, a makeup and henna artist based in New Jersey, serving clients across NJ and NYC.

My work sits at the intersection of beauty, culture, and artistry. I love creating makeup that enhances rather than hides, and henna designs that feel both timeless and personal.

Whether I'm creating a bride's wedding-day look or sitting down with a client for an intricate henna design, I believe the smallest details make the biggest difference.

From Paterson and Clifton to Wayne, Totowa, Haledon, and Garfield — I've had the joy of being part of some of the most special days in brides' lives across North New Jersey and New York City.

Behind the Artistry

Every Detail Matters

From the first brushstroke to the final setting spray, I approach every look with the same care and attention. Each bride is unique, and each look should be too.

I take the time to understand your features, your outfit, the lighting at your venue, and even how your photography will be styled — so every detail comes together beautifully.
